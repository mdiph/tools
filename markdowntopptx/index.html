Markdown → PowerPoint

Turns your course material (.md) into a designed .pptx deck. No account, no server, no AI.

runs 100% in your browser · nothing is uploaded

1 Load your markdown

Click to choose a .md file or drag it here
Jekyll / Chirpy style is supported: YAML front matter, quizzes, practice tables, tips, <details> solutions, mermaid flowcharts, images

2 Check the details

Title and subtitle come from the front matter (title, description). Change them here if you like; the outline below updates automatically. Image paths such as /assets/img/… are loaded from the course URL's site, so the page must be published. The site must allow downloads from other pages; GitHub Pages does.

Branding

The logo goes in the circle on the title slide. PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP; a transparent PNG or SVG looks best. Pick a light or mid-tone accent: text on accent shapes is dark. Branding is remembered in this browser.

3 Review the slide outline
